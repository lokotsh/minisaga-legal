Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

MiniSaga Studio ("we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to respecting and protecting the privacy of content creators who use our services. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, transmission, and protection when utilizing our software and TikTok API integration.

1. Information We Access & Collect

MiniSaga Studio interfaces with the TikTok Content Posting API via standard OAuth 2.0 authorization. We only access information that you explicitly authorize:

  • Basic Profile Identification: Your TikTok OpenID or display username to identify the active connected account.
  • Posting Permissions: Temporary authorized tokens allowing the scheduled upload of video drafts and series to your profile.

2. How Your Data Is Handled & Stored

  • Local / Secure Storage: Access tokens and configuration credentials are held securely within the application's environment configuration. We never transmit your private keys or tokens to unauthorized third parties.
  • Zero Third-Party Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker personal information, video content, or account metrics to any third parties or advertising networks.

3. Video Assets & Creation Privacy

All scripts, narrative series concepts, and generated media remain strictly confidential. Video assets are only published to TikTok upon your explicit scheduling and approval.

4. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • Revocation: You can disconnect MiniSaga Studio from your TikTok profile at any time by visiting your TikTok mobile app: Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
  • Data Deletion Request: If you wish to request the deletion of any stored tokens, cached data, or profile identifiers held by MiniSaga Studio, please email our support team. Requests will be executed within 48 hours.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, data requests, or support, please reach out to us at: support@minisagastudio.local
